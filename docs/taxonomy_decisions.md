# Taxonomic decisions needing qualification

This is a review snapshot of the curated crosswalk. Each row below has a broader or manually selected Catalogue of Life match, an approximate Avibase parent, or a Kenyan-specific assignment. The authoritative, editable details for all 1,067 taxa are in `data/curated/atlas_taxonomy.csv`, including source links and fuller notes. Other `name_matched` entries have not all been individually validated.

Catalogue of Life genus IDs are broad fallbacks, not equivalent species concepts. When sent to GBIF as `taxonConceptID`, they can cause GBIF to interpret a record at genus level or flag a conflict with its supplied species name. Parent concepts can overlap separate atlas SEQs; they do not merge observations.

## Corrections from the October 2026 coherence review

- SEQ 531: `avibase-6A0D6123`, the former Rufous-naped complex, includes the current Kenyan Rufous-naped and Athi Larks. The source account is printed p. 306 (scan 323); AviList's Corypha decisions place athi under former africana.
- SEQ 532: `avibase-BAA83B0C`, Red-winged/Kidepo Lark. Printed pp. 306–307 explicitly associate the Ugandan atlas records with kidepoensis; Athi is not a descendant of hypermetra. The national Kenyan checklist does not gain Kidepo from these Ugandan records.
- Both existing COL species matches are narrower than those historical groups and are withheld from occurrence `taxonConceptID` with `col_publish=false`. The broad Avibase IDs and original atlas identifications remain available.
- SEQ 780 retains the pre-2026 Spotted/Mediterranean Flycatcher concept. The new Spotted Flycatcher ID is appropriate for records identified to that child, not for all undifferentiated historical records.
